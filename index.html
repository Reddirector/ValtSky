The ValtSky Technical Pillars

Every line of code is engineered for zero compromise.

💎 100% Bit-for-Bit Lossless

Photos and videos are dispatched exclusively via Telegram's uncompressed document protocol. All EXIF metadata, camera tags, color profiles, and raw bitrates are 100% preserved.

⏳ Monotonic Chronological Sort

Photos upload from oldest to newest (dateTaken ASC). In your Telegram channel, your newest photos always stay right at the bottom, matching real-world chronology.

🚀 4-Click 2GB+ Oversized Vault

Telegram restricts bot uploads to 2GB max. ValtSky catches files $\ge$ 2GB in a dedicated vault and lets you send them manually to the same channel in 4 clicks.

🛡️ 98/100 Hardened Security

Magic bytes file header validation, strict Telegram MarkdownV2 entity escaping, SSRF localhost defense, and memory chunking prevent malicious payloads and leaks.

🛠️ 1-Click Self-Healing Diagnostics

Network dropped? Channel permissions missing? Battery optimizer killing background sync? Tap the Diagnostic Banner for instant root-cause analysis and automated resolution.

🎨 Monochrome Noir Motion

Obsidian base with surgical high-visibility alert accents, micro-interaction press springs, passive sine-wave socket monitors, and crossfade page transitions.

3-Minute Telegram Setup

ValtSky connects to your own private Telegram channel via your personal bot.

1
Create Your Bot via @BotFather

Open Telegram, search for @BotFather, send /newbot, name your bot, and copy your private Bot Token (e.g. 7123456789:AAFabcDEF...).

2
Create a Private Telegram Channel & Add Bot as Admin

Create a New Channel in Telegram, set it to Private, go to Channel Settings $\rightarrow$ Administrators $\rightarrow$ Add Administrator, search your bot, and grant Post Messages permission.

3
Find Your Channel ID & Paste into ValtSky

Forward any message from your channel to @userinfobot to retrieve your ID (e.g. -1001234567890). Paste the Bot Token and Channel ID into ValtSky and tap Test & Save Connection.
